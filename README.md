### 🔍 Research interests

My main interests lie at the intersection of **Computational Linguistics, Natural Language Processing and Artificial Intelligence**.

I enjoy combining my background in linguistics with programming and machine learning to develop practical solutions to language-related challenges.

---

### 🧠 Previous research

My previous research experience includes **Speech and Language Technologies**, particularly the creation of regional Spanish speech datasets and the adaptation of Text-to-Speech (TTS) models through Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) techniques such as LoRA.

Most of my projects involve Python and computational approaches to language, particularly:

- **Natural Language Processing:** text processing, corpus analysis and NLP pipelines.
- **Large Language Models:** Transformers, model adaptation, fine-tuning and evaluation.
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning:** neural networks, sequence modelling and representation learning.
- **Speech Technologies:** Text-to-Speech (TTS), speech datasets and acoustic analysis.
- **Linguistic Data Processing:** corpus construction, annotation and linguistic resource development.
